Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-15

The short version

An account is optional. You can log workouts, estimate your 1RM and use the strength ranks offline without signing in. Create an account only if you want cloud backup across devices, or to join the public standings or use friends.

If you create an account, you can use an email address and password, Apple, or Google sign-in.

You can delete your account and the training data stored with it from inside the app, at any time. Account deletion has exceptions: purchase records held by Apple or Google and by RevenueCat, the Apple Search Ads attribution record held by RevenueCat, and anonymous diagnostics (see Retention).

We do not sell your data, do not run ads, and do not share your data with advertisers.

What we store on your device

Your profile: name you choose, sex, age, bodyweight, unit preference and, if you enter it for nutrition targets, your height. Sex, age and bodyweight are required because strength thresholds are calibrated to them — without these the ranking is meaningless. Your hunter name is shown only to your friends and to people you send a friend request to; your letter and score are shown only if you join the public standings (see What we store in the cloud).

Your training log: exercise, weight, reps or duration, and the time of each set.

Body measurements, if you enter them: the circumferences you take with a tape measure (neck, chest, waist, hips, thigh, arm, calf) and the date of each reading. These never leave your device — the cloud does not store them — and the CSV export includes all of them.

Your settings: language, chosen movements, custom movements, and the template program you are following.

This data stays on your device unless you create an account.

What we store in the cloud (only if you create an account)

Your email address, used for sign-in and account recovery only. If you sign in with Apple or Google, the provider may also share your name, and Google may share a profile picture link; Supabase stores these with your sign-in record. The app does not display or use them.

The same profile and training log described above, including the selected bodyweight. Your age is stored as a birth year only (no day or month). If you choose to copy a bodyweight reading from Apple Health into your profile, that selected profile value is included in this account backup.

The date your 7-day trial started. It is tied to your account so the trial is not restarted by reinstalling the app; the account copy is deleted with your account. A copy of the date also stays on this device after you delete your account or reset the Awakening, so that resetting cannot restart the trial.

Public standings (off by default; only with an account). If you choose to join, other people using the app can see your overall rank letter, your power score and your place in the list, under a numbered label such as “Hunter #1234”. Your hunter name is not shown in the public list. Nothing else is shown: not your email address, bodyweight, age, sex or training log. Your friends may be able to recognise your entry in the public list by its score. To build the list, the server computes your rank letters from your synced sets and stores them with your entry, including letters per body region that are not shown. We also store when you joined or left, as a record of your choice. You can leave at any time in PROFILE → Public standings: your standings entry is deleted immediately. If you join again later, you get a new number. If you never join, you are never listed. Legal basis: your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a), because the letters are derived from health-related training data; explicit consent under KVKK), which you can withdraw at any time by leaving.

Friends (only if you use them; not available without an account). You can send a friend request with someone's friend code; you become friends only when they accept. The list of your friends and pending requests is stored with your account so the Friends tab can show their standings. Your friends and the people you send a friend request to see your hunter name. Your letter and score are shown, also in your friends' list, only if you have joined the public standings. When you send a friend request, the person you send it to sees your hunter name so they can decide; you do not see theirs until they accept. Your hunter name is whatever you typed; it does not have to be your real name, and you can change it in your profile. A request that is declined or withdrawn, and a friend you remove, is deleted for both of you at once, and your name is no longer shown to them. If you decline someone's request or remove them as a friend, they cannot send you a new request, even with a new code, unless you send them a request yourself. Separately, you can renew your friend code so the old one stops working; your referral code does not change. Legal basis: performance of our agreement with you, to provide the friends feature you choose to use (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

Fair-play checks (only if you join the public standings). While you take part in the public standings, the server checks your synced sets against two limits and stores a short record when a set crosses one. (1) If the weight is far beyond the strongest results on record for your bodyweight (a fixed ratio per body region, based on powerlifting records plus a 30% margin), the set is left out of the letter and score shown in the standings (public list and friends list), and your account is marked as having such a set until a later check finds none. The rank the app shows you on your device is not changed. (2) On bodyweight movements such as pull-ups and dips, if the added load is equal to or more than your bodyweight, the set is left out of your standings score only (public list and friends list). The record holds the movement, the values that were compared, the limit and the time. It is not shown to other people and is used only to keep the standings fair. These checks are automatic; if you think a set was left out by mistake, contact us. Leaving the public standings stops new checks but does not delete existing records; a record is deleted when you delete that set or your account. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the standings fair (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). These checks run only when you have joined the public standings; you can object to them, or stop them by leaving.

Hosting is provided by Supabase. Row Level Security is enabled: the database rejects any attempt to read another user's rows. The only exceptions are the public standings and friends described above: the standings return only the numbered label, rank letter, power score and place of people who chose to join, and your hunter name is shown only to your friends and to people you send a friend request to.

Diagnostics

The app records a small set of anonymous counters: a set was logged, a rank went up, a card was shared, an export was taken, plus the technical details of a crash. Each counter also carries the device's operating system (iOS or Android); your country and language are not sent.

While diagnostics are on, these counters leave your device. Anonymous crash and event counts are processed through PostHog, and crash reports are processed through Sentry so a crash can be traced to the file and line it came from; both store their data in the European Union (PostHog: eu.i.posthog.com, Sentry: ingest.de.sentry.io). The app does not include your IP address in what it sends. Neither service stores your IP address or keeps a location derived from it. Location data derived before 14 November 2026 is deleted when those records reach the end of their retention period. Records are tied to a random install identifier that belongs to the installation and not to your account, and it is gone when you uninstall the app. With diagnostics off, no diagnostic counters or crash reports are sent. The switch does not cover subscriptions (see Subscriptions and ad attribution) or update checks (see App updates).

They contain no name, no email address, no bodyweight and no free text. Only fixed values such as rank letters and body regions, plus counts and yes/no flags, are allowed through. This is enforced by the code, not by policy, and an automated test keeps it that way.

You can switch diagnostics off at any time: PROFILE then DIAGNOSTICS. From the moment you switch it off, no further diagnostic counters or crash reports are sent.

Subscriptions and ad attribution

Subscriptions are managed through RevenueCat, our subscription processor. RevenueCat receives your purchase and subscription history from Apple or Google and an app user ID: a random ID, or your account ID if you are signed in. RevenueCat is based in the United States, so this data is processed on servers outside Turkey and the EU.

Ad attribution (iOS, only while Diagnostics is on): the app sends RevenueCat a one-time Apple attribution token. RevenueCat exchanges it with Apple for a record of the Apple Search Ads ad that led to the install, if there was one: campaign, ad group, country or region, click date, conversion type and, when available, keyword. We use it only to see which ads lead to subscriptions. No advertising identifier (IDFA) is read and you are not tracked across apps. With Diagnostics off, no token is sent.

App updates

App updates are delivered through Expo (650 Industries, Inc., United States), the platform the app is built on, so this data may be processed outside Turkey and the EU. An update check sends Expo the platform (iOS or Android), the app's runtime version, the update channel, the identifiers of the installed and currently running app code, and a random installation identifier that Expo's update library creates on your device. If the app crashed while starting, the next check also includes that error message. As with any internet request, Expo's servers see your IP address; Expo handles it under its own privacy policy.

Update checks do not depend on the Diagnostics switch. From version 1.0.5 the app checks every time it starts: an available update downloads in the background and is applied the next time you open the app. Version 1.0.4 and earlier check when you tap CHECK FOR UPDATE in PROFILE, or after the app crashed while starting.

The coach

The built-in coach runs entirely on this device: it answers from the numbers the engine already computed, plus published sports-science ranges for protein. Nothing is sent anywhere.

The weekly reminder, if you enable it, is a local notification scheduled on the device. No push service and no server is involved, and it can be switched off in PROFILE at any time.

A future version may add an AI-powered coach. It is switched off in this version and sends nothing anywhere. The rest of this section describes what would happen if it is switched on later — it is here so the change is never a surprise.

Cloud backup for signed-in accounts also sends the profile and training log described above to Supabase. The AI coach is disabled in this version. If we offer it in a future version, it will send data to Anthropic only after a separate, explicit consent step.

What is sent: your rank letters, your progress percentage towards the next rank, your open quest targets in kilograms, your weekly set and session counts, your streak length, plateau warnings, and the question you typed.

What is not sent: your name, your email address, your bodyweight, your age, your sex, and your raw set log. Ranks are already normalised to bodyweight, so the coach can do its job without the number itself.

Your question and the context above are sent to Anthropic, which operates the language model, and are processed on servers outside Turkey and the EU. Anthropic does not use API data to train its models. We do not store your conversations on our servers; only a counter of how many messages you have sent is kept, so the usage limit can be applied.

Because training data is health-related data under GDPR Article 9, the coach asks for separate, explicit consent the first time you open it. Declining costs you nothing else in the app: every other feature keeps working exactly as before. You can withdraw consent at any time in PROFILE.

The coach is not a doctor, a physiotherapist or a personal trainer. It cannot diagnose anything and it will not prescribe medication or supplement doses.

Apple Health (iOS, optional)

Your rank is your estimated one-rep max divided by your bodyweight, so bodyweight is part of the calculation. On iOS you can let the app read your latest bodyweight from Apple Health instead of typing it in.

It is READ-ONLY and opt-in. Nothing is ever written to Apple Health, and the app asks for exactly one data type: body mass. No steps, no heart rate, no workouts.

When you choose a Health reading, the selected bodyweight is copied into your profile. While you are signed out it stays on this device; if you have an account, that profile value is included in the cloud backup described above. We do not use Apple Health data for advertising, tracking or analytics, and we never write data back to Apple Health.

The app is deliberately cautious with it: a reading older than 30 days, outside a plausible range, or more than 20 kg away from your current weight is not applied silently. You are asked first, because bodyweight changes every one of your six ranks.

Revoking access in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices stops future reads. A bodyweight already copied into your profile remains there until you edit it; if you have an account, it remains in your cloud profile until changed or your account is deleted.

Retention

Cloud data is kept until you delete your account, after which it is removed immediately by cascade deletion.

Public standings and friends: leaving the public standings deletes your standings entry immediately; fair-play records stay until you delete that set or your account. Friend links and pending requests are deleted as soon as a request is declined or withdrawn or a friend is removed; a block set by declining or removing stays until you send that person a request yourself. Your friend code is kept until you renew it. Each friend request you try to send, including ones with a wrong code, is logged with its time (not with who it was for) so the daily limit can be applied; entries older than a day are cleared the next time you send a request, and if you do not send another, they stay until you delete your account. All of these are deleted with your account.

Local data is kept until you delete your account, reset the Awakening, or uninstall the app. Resetting the Awakening clears your training log and profile; without an account it also clears measurements, nutrition settings, custom movements and programs, and with an account those stay on this device.

Account deletion has these exceptions. (1) Purchase records: Apple or Google keep your purchase history under their own policies, and RevenueCat keeps your purchase history until we delete it. (2) Ad attribution: if an Apple Search Ads record was created (see Subscriptions and ad attribution), it stays with RevenueCat until we delete it. You can ask us to delete your RevenueCat records. (3) Diagnostics: diagnostic counters are held by PostHog, and crash reports by Sentry, in the European Union for the retention period that applies to each project, and are deleted automatically at the end of it. The reason is structural: they are tied to a random install identifier with no link back to you, so there is nothing for a cascade delete to find. They hold no name, email address, bodyweight or free text. Switching diagnostics off in PROFILE stops diagnostic counters and crash reports from that moment.

Os dados na nuvem são mantidos até você excluir sua conta; depois disso, são removidos imediatamente por exclusão em cascata.

Classificação pública e amigos: sair da classificação pública exclui imediatamente sua entrada na classificação; os registros de jogo limpo ficam até você excluir essa série ou sua conta. Os dados locais são mantidos até você excluir sua conta, refazer o Awakening ou desinstalar o app. Refazer o Awakening apaga seu registro de treino e seu perfil; sem conta, também apaga medidas, configurações de nutrição, movimentos personalizados e programas, e com conta esses dados permanecem neste aparelho.

(1) Registros de compra: a Apple ou o Google mantêm seu histórico de compras conforme suas próprias políticas, e a RevenueCat mantém seu histórico de compras até que nós o excluamos.
