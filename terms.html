The short version

THE SYSTEM is a training log that assigns you a strength rank based on the weight you actually lift. It requires an account; once you are signed in the log works on your device, offline.

Some deeper features require a paid subscription. Your rank is never one of them: ranks cannot be bought, and no subscription changes the letter you are given.

This app is not a coach, a doctor or a physiotherapist. See the last section.

The AI coach

The AI coach is switched off in this version. The terms below apply if and when it is switched on.

The AI coach is an optional feature. It reads the numbers the ranking engine already produced for you and explains them in plain language.

The coach does not produce numbers of its own. Every weight, percentage and target it mentions comes from the engine; a reply that steps outside that boundary is discarded before you ever see it. This is enforced by code and by an automated test, not by a promise.

The coach cannot change your rank. No subscription and no conversation moves a letter.

Replies are generated by a language model and can still be wrong, incomplete or poorly judged. Treat them as a second opinion on your own data, not as instructions.
